01 / Our mission

Who are we?

Marina Sanctuary is a non-governmental organisation dedicated to rescuing and protecting wild mammals in Bulgaria. Our main goal is to establish a specialist rescue centre where injured, sick and orphaned wild animals can receive the medical treatment, care and rehabilitation they need.

Our aim is for every animal capable of living independently to return to its natural habitat. We believe that wildlife conservation also begins with understanding animals, which is why we want to raise public awareness and encourage responsible attitudes towards the natural world.

In memory of Marina

A dream we carry forward.

Marina Sanctuary honours a person who devoted her life to wildlife. Through this initiative, we carry her work forward and strive to realise her dream of a specialist rescue centre.
