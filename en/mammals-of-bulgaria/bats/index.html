Wildlife help

Found a bat on the ground or indoors in Bulgaria?

Bats are flying mammals, not birds. Bulgaria has many species, and exact identification often requires a specialist. Safety and avoiding direct contact come first.

I found a wild animal – interactive guidance

What to do in different situations

Bat indoors

Keep children and pets away. Do not strike it or handle it with bare hands. Obtain guidance for the particular room.

Bat on the ground

Do not toss it into the air. Contact a bat specialist or regional environmental inspectorate.

Bite or possible saliva exposure

Wash the area with soap and water and seek immediate medical assessment.

First steps for wildlife in distress

  1. Keep people and pets away. Do not attempt to make it fly.
  2. For a grounded, indoor or distressed bat, seek a bat specialist through a rescue centre or RIEW.
  3. If bitten, scratched or possibly exposed to saliva, wash the area and seek urgent medical assessment. Contact a vet if a pet was exposed.
Important

This is general guidance. Marina Sanctuary is developing a rescue centre and does not announce admission for every listed species. For urgent wildlife cases contact an appropriate specialist or RIEW; 112 is for immediate danger to people.
