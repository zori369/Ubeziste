Wildlife help

Bear, wolf, lynx or wildcat in Bulgaria: safety and reporting

An encounter with a wild predator does not justify capturing it. Human safety takes priority around large carnivores. This page is not a guide to handling them.

I found a wild animal – interactive guidance

What to do in different situations

Bear or wolf near people

Keep your distance and retreat safely without approaching the animal. Call 112 if people are in immediate danger.

Injured or trapped large predator

Do not attempt to free it. Send its location to the competent authorities.

Wildcat or kitten

Appearance alone may not reliably distinguish it from a domestic cat. Request specialist identification.

First steps for wildlife in distress

  1. Do not chase, capture or feed it.
  2. If in its normal habitat with no signs of distress, leave it alone. A lone juvenile does not prove orphaning.
  3. If injured, trapped or possibly distressed, contact a specialist or RIEW with a location and distant photo.
Important

This is general guidance. Marina Sanctuary is developing a rescue centre and does not announce admission for every listed species. For urgent wildlife cases contact an appropriate specialist or RIEW; 112 is for immediate danger to people.
