Wildlife help

Found a fawn or baby hare in Bulgaria?

Young deer and hares may spend extended periods alone while their mothers are away. This is normal behaviour and not proof of orphaning.

I found a wild animal – interactive guidance

What to do in different situations

Healthy fawn in grass

Leave without touching it. Do not wait beside it for its mother.

Baby hare

Do not take it home just because it is still or appears alone. A wild hare is not a pet.

Mowing or road collision

Report the case before attempting relocation. Call 112 if people are in immediate danger.

First steps for wildlife in distress

  1. Leave promptly and keep people and dogs away.
  2. Do not remain nearby waiting for the mother; your presence may deter her.
  3. If the mother is known dead, the young is injured or in danger, contact a specialist before intervening.
Important

This is general guidance. Marina Sanctuary is developing a rescue centre and does not announce admission for every listed species. For urgent wildlife cases contact an appropriate specialist or RIEW; 112 is for immediate danger to people.
