Wildlife help

Found a stranded dolphin or porpoise on Bulgaria's Black Sea coast?

The Black Sea is home to bottlenose dolphins, common dolphins and harbour porpoises. Stranded marine mammals require a different specialist response from terrestrial wildlife.

I found a wild animal – interactive guidance

What to do in different situations

Live stranded dolphin

Do not drag it by its fins or return it to the sea without guidance. Keep people and dogs back and notify the competent authorities.

Dead marine mammal

Do not touch it. Record the place, date and a distant photograph and report it to the regional environmental inspectorate.

Animal in the water

Do not enter the sea if doing so puts you or others at risk.

First steps for wildlife in distress

  1. Do not handle it, even if it seems calm.
  2. Record the precise location, time, visible symptoms and any cat or dog contact. Take a photo from a safe distance.
  3. Contact RIEW or a wildlife organisation and share the information.
Important

This is general guidance. Marina Sanctuary is developing a rescue centre and does not announce admission for every listed species. For urgent wildlife cases contact an appropriate specialist or RIEW; 112 is for immediate danger to people.
