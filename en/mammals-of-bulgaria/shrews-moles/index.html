Wildlife help

Found a shrew or mole in Bulgaria?

Shrews and moles are not rodents. Shrews may resemble mice but have different care needs. Do not try to raise a found animal based on a guess about its identity.

I found a wild animal – interactive guidance

What to do in different situations

Shrew brought in by a cat

Report the contact to a specialist even if no injury is visible.

Mole above ground

Do not place it in an arbitrary burrow. Seek advice if you think it is in distress.

Small insectivorous mammal

Do not apply feeding schedules for domestic rodents. Their requirements can be very different.

First steps for wildlife in distress

  1. Keep your distance. Even small animals can bite. Tell the specialist the species, size and behaviour.
  2. Only under specific safe containment instructions, use a suitable ventilated container away from noise, children and pets.
  3. Do not give food, water by mouth, milk or medication. Do not treat wounds or manipulate limbs.
  4. Record the precise location, time, visible symptoms and any cat or dog contact. Take a photo from a safe distance.
Important

This is general guidance. Marina Sanctuary is developing a rescue centre and does not announce admission for every listed species. For urgent wildlife cases contact an appropriate specialist or RIEW; 112 is for immediate danger to people.
